What Drives Me

I am a bioinformatician with a background in pharmacy, driven by a deep curiosity to decode the hidden mechanisms behind disease. The more complex the data, the more exciting the challenge.

I am especially passionate about spatial omics, where large-scale molecular data meets structure, image, and location. It’s not just data analysis; it’s exploration in multiple dimensions.

Beyond research, I enjoy visual storytelling: from image design and video creation to crafting interactive websites. For me, science and creativity are not separate, they amplify each other.
